# Mod ideas backlog

Ideas saved for later exploration. Nothing here is planned or built yet. When an idea is picked up, it moves to `FEATURES.md` as **Planned**.

Several of these overlap with the **Loot system** and **Combat mechanics** entries already planned in `FEATURES.md`.

## Inventory and equipment

- **Backpack**, World of Warcraft style: extra bag storage you carry with you.
- **Equipment slots and inventory tabs**:
  - dedicated slots for gear instead of keeping everything in the main grid;
  - tabs to organise the inventory.

## Powers

- **Power wheel**: a radial menu for picking active powers.
  - Includes powers granted by items, e.g. a belt of strength giving unlimited carry weight.

## Loot and endgame

- **Corruption shards**: an item that corrupts other items.
- **Custom dungeons**: like Diablo IV's dungeons and Diablo II's maps.
- **Custom crafting bench** for making those dungeon maps and corrupted items.

## Progression

- **Skill system redesign**: instead of a skill levelling up automatically as you use it, you earn levels and stat points and choose where to spend them.

## Creatures

- **Anti-aggro talismans**:
  - Crafted at a workbench from a specific monster's trophies.
  - While equipped, that monster is repelled.
- **Open questions:**
  - Allow two talismans equipped at once?
  - Should magic rings work the same way, or be a separate item type?
  - Theme talismans and rings on Viking lore?
